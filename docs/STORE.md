## 二、短描述（商店页顶部，≤ 300 字符）

**中文**

> 一种围棋：一手可以同时落在两个点上，棋盘当场裂成两个宇宙。两边各有一颗子可能根本不存在，谁也不知道是哪颗。
> 提掉它，那个宇宙就消失。本地双人、在线对战、15 分钟一局。

**English**

> A Go variant where one move can land on two points at once, splitting the board into two universes.
> One stone in each may not exist at all, and neither player knows which. Capture it and that universe collapses.
> Local and online two-player.

## 三、长描述

**中文**

量子围棋把围棋的棋盘变成了一棵可能性之树。

落一手普通棋，和围棋没有区别：气、提子、劫，都按围棋的规矩来。

落一手量子步，你要同时点两个空点。现实当场裂成两个宇宙，各占一半可能性：两个宇宙里各落一颗子，两边都继续下。

关键在于：**这两颗子里恰好有一颗是虚的，谁也不知道是哪颗。** 双方看到的是同样的棋盘，但你永远不知道自己下的那颗是不是真的。提掉那颗虚子是一次观测——那个宇宙连同它下面所有分支一起消失，可能性交给另一个宇宙。

所以每一局你都在赌：赌对手哪颗子是虚的，赌自己该在哪个世界里用力。

对局结束后可以**复盘**，逐步回看，那时候才标出当时哪些子是虚的。

**你会得到**

- **一局真正的棋**：数地、提子、劫争、死子标记，按加权目数结算，不是换个皮的井字棋。
- **两种对局方式**：同一台电脑两个人，或者联机对战（局域网或直连，不需要账号）。
- **五步互动教程**：用真棋盘带你走一遍分裂与坍缩，三分钟上手。
- **本地排行榜**：每局结束自动记录，Elo 积分、战绩、最近对局。

**适合谁**

喜欢围棋但嫌 19 路太长的、喜欢抽象棋类但想要点新东西的、想找个能和对象/朋友下一局的。

**English**

Quantum Go turns the Go board into a tree of possibilities.

A normal move is just Go: liberties, captures, ko, all unchanged.

A **quantum move** lands on two empty points at once. Reality splits: the universe you were playing in is retired and replaced by two siblings, half the weight each, one stone in each. Both keep playing.

The catch: **exactly one of those two stones is phantom — it may not exist at all — and neither player knows which.** You both see the same boards, but you never know whether your own stone is real. Capturing the phantom is an observation: that universe collapses together with everything grown out of it, and its weight passes to the sibling.

So every game is a bet — on which of your opponent's stones is phantom, and on which world is worth your moves.

When the match is over you can **step through a replay** and only then see which stones were phantoms.

So every game is a decision about which world to play in.

**What you get**

- **An actual game**: territory scoring, captures, ko, dead-stone marking, weighted totals — not a reskin.
- **Two ways to play**: two players on one PC, or online (LAN or direct IP, no account needed).
- **A five-step interactive tutorial**: the real board, the real rules engine, three minutes.
- **A local leaderboard**: every finished match is recorded with Elo, record and recent games.

**Who it's for**

Go players who want a shorter board, abstract-strategy players who want something new, and anyone looking for a game to play with a friend.

## 六、Steamworks 后台配置

1. **AppID**：建好后把 `src/game/qg_platform.gd` 的 `STEAM_APP_ID` 从 480 换成正式值，
   并在打包目录放一个内容为 AppID 的 `steam_appid.txt`（`tools/build_windows.ps1` 会自动写）。
2. **成就**（名称必须与代码里的常量一致）：

| API 名称 | 显示名（建议） | 触发条件 | 隐藏 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `FIRST_QUANTUM_SPLIT` | 第一次分裂 | 第一次落量子步 | 否 |
| `FIRST_COLLAPSE` | 第一次观测 | 第一次提掉幻影子 | 否 |
| `WIN_FIRST_MATCH` | 首胜 | 赢下第一局 | 否 |

3. **排行榜**：建一个名为 `TOTAL_WEIGHTED_SCORE` 的排行榜，类型 Highest Score（降序），
   写入模式 Keep Best。游戏会在每局结束时提交自己的加权目数。
4. **云存档**：同步 `user://settings.cfg` 与 `user://leaderboard.json`。
   Steam 的根目录分别是 `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Quantum Go\` 下同名文件。
5. **启动选项**：临时包用 `QuantumGo.exe --main-pack QuantumGo.pck`；正式导出后清空参数。
6. **Controller**：当前只支持键鼠，商店页不要写「支持手柄」。
